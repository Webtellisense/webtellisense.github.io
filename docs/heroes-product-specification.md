# Heroes product explanation

New main-based branch: `feat/heroes-product-specification`. Baseline: deployed main `ec5d24a90138a040b4d4f14b28452d20e906fa26`. This is a focused product-explanation change, not a continuation or redesign of PR #5. No merge or deployment was performed.

## Customer-facing change

The former four-paragraph responsibility section is replaced by:

1. **Your digital presence. Taken care of.** Eight connected responsibilities, explained through a search preview, business/content imagery, an offer-to-enquiry example and the structure of a monthly report. Four paired editorial compositions avoid eight repeated service cards. Existing contextual service links remain.
2. **The Heroes standard.** Five short comparisons explain purposeful social, search, brand, advertising and reporting work. Native disclosures keep the detail available by keyboard and without JavaScript. The existing illustrative collaboration image is retained; it is not labelled as Webtellisense staff.
3. **Compare what’s included.** Foundation, Growth and Complete explain customer fit, progression, content/search/acquisition depth, enquiry handling and support. Desktop shows all three; mobile has an independent native tier selector. Without JavaScript all three are readable. Shared support and the scope qualifier remain visible outside the selector.

The existing pricing section is byte-for-byte unchanged. New links lead to that section without altering its currently selected tier or billing period. All nine totals, included advertising allocations, prepaid agency-fee discounts, payment configuration, manual verification, callback policy, portfolio, onboarding and existing CTAs are preserved. The FAQ’s tier answer and its JSON-LD now match the new comparison.
