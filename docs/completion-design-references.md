# Design and source review

Inspected the stored Heroes first-screen, desktop/mobile, welcome and hosted-handoff previews in `docs/integration-previews/` before editing. Baseline copies are in `completion-previews/before/`. Source review included index, about, services, pricing/packages, the complete portfolio image set, client logos, local fonts and earlier Heroes code. No attached Socialander screenshots were available in the file library; they were not claimed as inspected.

Public web-reader research on 7 November 2026:

- [Socialander marketing](https://socialander.com/marketing/): direct service explanation, grouped capabilities and repeated consultation paths. Borrowed the principle of showing why a service matters; no reviews, numbers, partnerships or copy were transferred.
- [Socialander transportation](https://socialander.com/transportation-digital-marketing-company/): sector context before channel recommendations. New industry pages explain different customer decisions; they do not recycle transportation content or claim unsupported sector results.
- [MTN Nigeria](https://www.mtn.ng/) and [FibreX](https://www.mtn.ng/broadband/fibrex/): clear product/plan language, benefits before plan choice, prices/periods and practical FAQs. The existing strong Heroes comparison is preserved byte-for-byte. The initially attempted `/fibrex/` timed out; the actual linked broadband route was read.
- [MTN X](https://www.mtn.ng/helppersonal/mtn-x/): straightforward eligibility/how-it-works questions. No MTN promises were adapted into Webtellisense scope.
- [Nestuge Plus](https://nestuge.com/plus): a unified product story and human business context. No sub-brands or invented customer proof were introduced.
- [Selar](https://selar.com/) returned 403 to the reader. Its [official selling guide](https://selar.com/blog/how-to-sell-online/) was accessible and informed the concise explanation of steps. This is not a claim to have visually inspected its blocked homepage.

These were structural/content observations through the web reader, not pixel-perfect browser inspections or access to the reference sites’ private metrics. Local before/after screenshots establish the actual Webtellisense design changes.

Implemented around the preserved comparison and portfolio: existing relatable shop imagery, layered navy treatment, explicitly illustrative enquiry notes, small editorial photo compositions, a real founder portrait, collaboration photography clearly distinguished from a staff portrait, visual delivery steps, one-time restrained strip movement and a user-controlled slideshow. No heavy dependency or generated imagery. Reduced motion removes animation; no-JS retains the content, prices, contact routes, profile form and native horizontal portfolio scrolling.

New WebP derivatives and known rights limitations are recorded in [the image manifest](completion-image-provenance.json). No newly downloaded/licensed Unsplash asset was actually used. An individual free-license candidate was researched but its download was blocked by the managed network policy. Existing library photographs have no original license receipts in this repository; confirm those before production reuse rather than claiming rights clearance we do not have.
