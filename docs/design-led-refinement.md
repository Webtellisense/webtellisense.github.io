## What changed

**Heroes.** One photographic composition per viewport replaces the disconnected backdrop/floating photograph arrangement. Desktop uses the higher-resolution existing commercial workspace; mobile uses a deliberately cropped shop-owner scene with the face above the headline and navy fade behind the copy. The five-message demonstration cycles through enquiry, consultation, quote, callback and order questions. Each message enters, leaves and is replaced rather than stacking permanently. It is explicitly illustrative, never live customer proof or a sales result. Native controls pause/advance it; hover, keyboard focus, hidden document, offscreen position and reduced motion suspend playback. Without JavaScript one message remains readable. The message area reserves its height.

“Less chasing” now connects a shop scene with a three-step discovery → clear information → conversation demonstration. “People doing the work” gives collaboration photography a full visual role alongside visible service responsibilities. The four onboarding stages have distinct photographs, readable gradient-backed summaries and native expandable details usable by touch, mouse and keyboard. No essential information depends on hover. The collaboration image is honestly described as illustrative, not a Webtellisense team portrait.

The reviewed **pricing section is byte-for-byte unchanged**, including all nine prices, period/tier interactions and CTA logic. The four-slide portfolio composition/content is also unchanged. No new project material was invented or published without permission. [Portfolio evidence and requested material](completion-portfolio-review.md).

**Industries.** The eight agreed sectors now form a varied photographic mosaic with a business problem, clear link and restrained hover/focus movement. Solar, trades, learning, retail, packaged food and hospitality use matching available imagery; fitness uses a temporary human-connection image that needs a stronger sector replacement. Focused workflows, experience-led scope and selective briefs remain distinguished. Hospitality, fitness and NGO links reach substantive hub briefs rather than newly invented thin pages. Construction remains an accessible additional context.

**Education.** A positive school story replaces the defensive narrative: discovery, school website, useful school content, enquiry/visit/application, and staff follow-through. The five-step viewer contains labelled conceptual website, social-post, enquiry and admissions examples. None is presented as client work, a ranking, real message or delivered portal; none collects information. Without JavaScript all five examples are readable and the inactive selector is hidden. School websites and **separately scoped admissions portals** are presented positively, with exact scope/access/data responsibilities in the FAQ/proposal context. Approved Heroes entitlements are not expanded.

Solar, technical services, retail, food and construction pages retain their useful original explanations and gain sector-specific conceptual website/customer journeys and photographic brief sections. They explain the actual customer decision without fabricating results. The education page has a distinct, deeper composition; the other pages share a coherent service-family treatment.

**Homepage, limited scope.** Replace the overcrowded header with Services / Industries / Our Work / Company / Heroes / Talk to Us. Services, packages, pricing, blog, reviews and FAQs remain reachable. The same coherent navigation appears on every industry page; Heroes keeps its focused conversion header. Native disclosures work without JavaScript; enhanced mobile Menu and Escape controls work with JavaScript. The old text-heavy industry introduction becomes three compact photographic sector links and an all-eight destination. Mobile hero type, image crop, overlay and carousel-control placement are corrected. LinkedIn is retained in the footer, and the existing AOS sections remain readable without JavaScript. Other homepage sections, claims, offers and functional integrations are preserved.
