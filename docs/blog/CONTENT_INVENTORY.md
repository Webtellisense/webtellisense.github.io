### Priority overlap decisions

Retain `/how-to-grow-your-business-online-2026` as the evergreen customer-acquisition pillar and route owner for item 14. Update title/scope only when the article actually covers the broader promise, preserving the Uyo evidence and current URL. Its existing body starts with a running business whose friend/referral network has run out, not a prospective founder seeking business ideas. Items 20 and 21 therefore warrant startup-opportunity coverage, with geography supported by material research rather than city-name substitution. Neither should be merged into the acquisition pillar.

The 205-word Eket ten-tips article is the other broad overlap: complete/narrow it into a concise local online-presence checklist and link to the pillar; do not create another generic acquisition article under item 14. The visibility article retains “why can't people find me?” diagnostic intent; item 25 serves the separate platform-choice decision. Detailed subtopic guides (#7, #10, #12, #13, #24) should expand beyond the pillar's summaries and link back, rather than repeat its five steps.

No existing published article is a clear deletion/redirect candidate based on code alone. Do not merge live URLs without traffic/backlink evidence; draft consolidation for #20/#21 is the safe conditional merge. New slugs are intentionally not treated as existing internal-link destinations: until published and verified, only the route table in `AUDIT.md` is the article-link allowlist. Content requiring current regulator, grant, tax, tariff, election, procurement or pricing facts must obtain primary dated evidence during writing; the repository supplies none of those external facts.
