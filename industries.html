A product post is not a site inspection.

Retail, beauty, food and hospitality can depend on frequent product updates, changing availability and trend-sensitive social content. A solar installer may need more explanation of the process, service area and site assessment before someone is ready to act. Schools have admissions calendars and safeguarding responsibilities. Each needs a different brief, approval process and publishing rhythm.
