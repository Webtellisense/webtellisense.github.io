Answer the questions families actually have.

Which classes are available? Where is the school? How do admissions work? Can a parent arrange a visit? When those answers are scattered across old posts and messages, a family can struggle to work out the next step. A clear website and current local information give your school a more useful starting point.

We consider the school’s real admissions process and the people responsible for answering enquiries. Our experience includes school social media management; it is not a claim that every school needs the same posting schedule or paid campaign. See the practical features of a school website for information families commonly need.

A school’s story needs care.

A useful admissions website.

Organise programmes, location, contact options and the agreed admissions steps. Confirm fees and dates with the school before publishing them. Explore website design for the underlying service; this page does not promise an admissions portal or new website within a Heroes tier.

Consistent, responsible school content.

Social media management can help explain school life and share useful updates. The school needs an approval process for photographs, names and stories, especially where children appear. No pupil images or personal information should be published without the appropriate permissions and review.

Accurate local information.

Keep opening/contact information and location details consistent through local visibility work. Families looking around Eket, Uyo and other communities need accurate information, not a page pretending the school has a campus where it does not.

Plan around the school calendar.

Term dates, admissions periods and school events should shape the content brief. Agree who provides source material, who checks the details, which images may be used and how enquiries are handled. If paid promotion is appropriate, discuss audience, budget and the destination page before launching it.

We can bring those agreed activities together through Heroes, or discuss a separate project. Advertising cannot guarantee enrolment: a family’s decision also depends on the school’s offer, suitability, location, capacity and admissions experience.

A few practical questions.

Can you guarantee admissions or enrolment?

No. We can improve the clarity and consistency of agreed marketing work. Enrolment remains a family decision and depends on the school and its admissions process.

Can you publish pupil photographs?

Only after the school confirms the appropriate permission and safeguarding review for the intended use. Do not send confidential pupil records through a marketing enquiry form.

Does Heroes automatically include a school website or portal?

No separate website entitlement or portal is promised here. Confirm your tier’s exact scope with Sales; one-off work can be quoted separately.

What should we prepare for a first conversation?

Bring your public website and social links, key admissions questions, upcoming dates and priorities. You do not need to share account passwords or pupil records.

One project, or a team alongside you?

If you need one piece of work, we can quote it separately. If your digital work needs ongoing coordination, explore ongoing support through Heroes. The right scope starts with a conversation, not an identical package for every industry.
