A few practical
questions.

Can you build an admissions portal?

Yes. We can scope a school website and a separate admissions portal around the process, access roles and data requirements you need. Exact functionality, costs and responsibilities are agreed in the proposal.

Is a new website or portal automatically included in Heroes?

Confirm the exact tier scope with Sales. One-off website work, admissions portals and add-ons can be quoted separately; this page does not change approved Heroes inclusions or pricing.

Can you work with our school photographs and content?

Yes, with appropriate permission and school approval, especially where children appear. The examples above are concepts using illustrative photography. They are not shown as a delivered client website or admissions result.

Can marketing guarantee enrolment?

No. Families’ decisions depend on the school’s offer, suitability, location, capacity and admissions experience as well as marketing. We agree the work without guaranteeing enrolment.
