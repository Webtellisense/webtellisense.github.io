Information you choose to share

Enquiries and contact
Forms, email and WhatsApp conversations may include your name, business, email address, telephone number and message, depending on the channel and form. The purpose is to respond to the enquiry and discuss the requested work.
Optional Heroes business profile
The Netlify form asks for a business name and contact email, with optional public website/social link and priorities. It is not required before your first call, and does not establish payment. Do not include passwords, recovery codes, API keys, banking details or confidential customer records.
Newsletter and guide requests
Existing forms request an email address, and some guide flows also request business/contact details before opening WhatsApp. Any marketing subscription practices and withdrawal process need operational confirmation.
Business Audit Tool
The tool uses answers and a business name to display a browser-generated assessment. Its optional website check sends the submitted website address through AllOrigins. Do not enter a private URL or one containing access tokens. A result does not prove it was stored or emailed; the current storage destination is unconfigured.
Payments
Paystack handles payment details on its checkout. Webtellisense needs transaction and customer contact information to reconcile payment and discuss the service. Heroes activation follows a manual Paystack check; a redirect or welcome page is not a receipt.

Analytics, advertising and choices

Existing pages include Google Analytics (G-PS5FNQCJRN), some Google Tag Manager installations and Meta pixels. They can involve cookies, online identifiers, device/network information and interaction events. The account configuration and a consistent sitewide consent approach remain release-review items; this draft does not claim the legacy website already applies consent gating everywhere.

The new Heroes and industry-page measurement module is off pending owner/privacy approval. It is designed to load optional providers only after a choice, to separate analytics and advertising choices and to respect the browser’s Global Privacy Control. When enabled, a “Measurement choices” control allows withdrawal. The browser stores that preference; it does not store your profile answers as an analytics preference.

Only plan-period/tier choices and contact/handoff actions are defined for Heroes. Form values, names, emails, telephone numbers, payment references and URL query strings are excluded from those event parameters. A Paystack handoff is not a purchase, successful lead or revenue event. Device/network identifiers can still be processed by enabled providers; the collection is not described as anonymous.

Before enabling the module, confirm the owned destinations, disable unwanted automatic/enhanced event collection in the provider accounts and approve the notice and consent approach. Existing third-party embeds and legacy tags need their own review.

Purposes, lawful bases and retention

Responding to a requested enquiry, arranging an agreed service, reconciling payment, website operation and optional marketing/measurement are different purposes. Nigeria’s Data Protection Act 2023 requires an appropriate lawful basis and transparent information for each. The owner/legal reviewer must confirm the basis applicable to each actual processing activity rather than treating all website use as consent.

Retention periods for enquiries, profiles, financial records, messages, subscriptions and analytics are not yet confirmed in this draft. They must be set by purpose and legal obligations, including how deletion and provider backups are handled. No invented deletion deadline or refund-related retention rule is published here.

Your choices and privacy requests

Under applicable Nigerian data-protection law, rights can include being informed, access, correction, erasure, restriction, objection, portability and withdrawal of consent where processing relies on consent, subject to legal conditions. Send a request through the published email above. Do not send identity documents or sensitive credentials in an initial message; any necessary verification should be arranged securely.

You can also contact the Nigeria Data Protection Commission about a data-protection concern. The review should confirm the business’s handling process and applicable statutory response obligations. This draft does not promise an unapproved internal response deadline.
