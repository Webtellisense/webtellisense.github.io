Ask us something else ↗
What exactly is Heroes?

Heroes is Webtellisense’s unified outsourced digital growth team. We bring the planning and execution of your agreed digital work into one relationship, so you don’t have to coordinate several providers.

What is included in my tier?

We’ll agree the delivery scope, priorities and responsibilities with you before activation. Websites, brand, search, content, social marketing and customer enquiry workflows are discussed as part of that plan. Ask Sales to confirm the exact scope for V1, V2 or V3; this page does not promise fixed quantities or a separate new-website entitlement.

Is the advertising allocation included?

Yes. Every price shown includes the monthly advertising allocation. Discounts on three- and six-month prepaid options apply to agency fees only. We’ll confirm your allocation and campaign priorities personally; it is not an extra charge added to the displayed total.

How do monthly and prepaid payments work?

Monthly uses a Paystack-managed recurring subscription where supported. Three and six months are fixed-amount, one-time prepaid purchases with manual renewal at the end of the period. Review the exact tier, NGN amount and renewal details on the hosted Paystack page before paying.

What if the payment link is unavailable?

Call or WhatsApp us and we’ll help arrange the correct Heroes option. We keep unconfigured links disabled. Please don’t use a standalone package checkout to pay for Heroes.

What happens after I pay?

Webtellisense manually checks the successful transaction, amount, tier and billing option directly in Paystack before activating service. A return link or welcome page is not payment confirmation. Our first call welcomes you and arranges your full 30–60-minute onboarding conversation.

When will the team contact me?

Proactive callbacks run every day, 8 AM–7 PM Africa/Lagos. After manual payment confirmation during that window, we target a callback in 1–2 hours. Late-evening follow-up may continue from 8 AM the next morning. After hours, callbacks begin from 8 AM next morning. You can call or message anytime; a human answer overnight isn’t guaranteed.

Can I ask for a separate project or a custom scope?

Yes. Custom Heroes scope starts with a Sales conversation. One-off projects and add-ons are quoted separately and agreed before work begins. There is no shopping cart or automatic addition to your subscription.
