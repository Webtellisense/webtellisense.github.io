RETAIL & SPECIALTY STORES

Give people a reason
to choose your retail store.

For relevant businesses in Eket, Uyo, Akwa Ibom and across Nigeria: A stationery shop, perfume store or beauty business needs useful product information, a recognisable presence and a clear route from interest to an enquiry or visit.

Talk through your priorities ↗Start with a business audit

Fresh information earns its place.

Customers want to know what you stock, where to find you and how to ask about availability. An old price post or unanswered message can make a good store harder to buy from. Local retail needs current information as well as attractive content.

Webtellisense’s experience includes Google Business Profile/local visibility work for a stationery business and social content for a perfume store. Those are different pieces of work, rather than evidence of an identical retail package. We start with what your store sells, how stock changes and how customers place an enquiry.

Be easy to find locally.

Keep the business name, address, opening information and contact options accurate. Google Business Profile management supports that local information. Read why a social page alone may not make a business easy to find for the wider discovery problem.

Show the product clearly.

Product images, prices where confirmed, carousels and short video can answer different questions. A perfume audience may need product explanations and frequent new content; a stationery customer may be looking for specific supplies or a convenient location. Our social media management and graphic design work can support an agreed publishing plan.

Make the purchase conversation straightforward.

Explain whether someone should visit, call or message about stock and delivery. Keep availability and fulfilment details with the people who manage the business. The right retail website information can give posts and profiles a dependable place to send customers.

Agree the content rhythm with the store.

Retail and beauty can call for more frequent, trend-sensitive content than a home-service business. That requires source material, prompt product/price checks and a clear approval process. We agree cadence, formats, responsibilities and any paid promotion before work starts.

A previous perfume engagement included frequent product posts, carousels and reels. We agree a format mix and publishing rhythm that suits your product range and the fresh material available. Campaign budget, production requirements and exact delivery scope need to be confirmed for your store. We do not claim that a post guarantees a sale or that a customer enquiry proves a purchase.

Before we start.

Do you need a shop with many products?

No fixed catalogue size is promised here. We discuss your range, stock changes, customer questions and available product material, then agree a suitable scope.

Can you keep every price and stock item updated?

Responsibilities and update frequency must be agreed. Your business needs to confirm prices, availability, delivery and fulfilment information before publication.

Does this include an online store?

A website, product catalogue, ecommerce build or other separate project must be agreed explicitly. They are not automatically added to a Heroes subscription.

Can we supply product photographs?

Yes, where you have the rights and permission to use them. Confirm permission for customer photographs and testimonials too; no stock subject will be presented as a customer.
