7 Must-Have Features for School Websites in Akwa Ibom

  • Webtellisense Team
  • March 19, 2024

Your school's website is often the first impression for prospective parents — and increasingly, it's the deciding factor in whether they call or move on to the next result. After designing websites for schools across Eket, Uyo, and other cities in Nigeria including Lagos, we've identified these seven essential features every school website needs:

1. Clear Admission Information

Include downloadable forms, requirements, and deadlines. St. Mary's Primary saw 40% more inquiries after simplifying their admissions section.

2. Mobile-Friendly Calendar

Parents should easily access term dates, exams, and events. Sync with Google Calendar for automatic updates.

3. Faculty Directory

Showcase your qualified staff with bios and photos to build trust. This helped Heritage Academy attract better teaching candidates.

"Our new website reduced front office queries by 60%, giving staff more time for students." - Principal Udoh, Excel Nursery School

4. Online Fee Payment Portal

Letting parents pay school fees, uniform costs, or excursion fees directly through the website removes a major source of front-desk congestion and delayed payments. A simple, secure payment integration is one of the highest-value additions a school can make to its site.

5. News & Announcements Section

A dedicated, regularly updated news feed keeps parents informed about closures, achievements, and upcoming events without relying on WhatsApp broadcasts or printed notices that get lost or missed.

6. Search Functionality & Clear Navigation

Parents looking for exam results, fee structures, or contact details shouldn't have to dig through a cluttered menu. A search bar and a simple, well-labelled navigation menu make the site usable for parents of all levels of digital comfort.

7. Mobile-Responsive Design

Most parents in Nigeria — whether in Akwa Ibom, Lagos, or elsewhere — will browse your school's website from a phone, not a laptop. A site that isn't fully responsive on mobile will lose prospective parents before they even see your admissions page.

Whether you're running a nursery school in Eket or a private academy in Lagos, getting these seven features right turns your website into a genuine enrollment tool rather than a digital brochure nobody reads.
